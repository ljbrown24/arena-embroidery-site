Los Angeles : studio, est. 2025
New York City : shop, est. 2018

arena Embroidery, Inc. is a specialty embellishment company with locations in New York City and Los Angeles. Since 2012 and incorporating in 2018, we service the fashion industry, as well as others like fine art and robotics. We specialize in sampling and prototyping using advanced embroidery applications, but no job is too big and no task is too small. No matter where a client is in development, we are willing to join you and your team in the process of getting a project to the finish line.
